Taikojiro skin "original", as used while developing Taiko Pocket: the images of the
"original" skin of a Taikojiro install, and in snd/ that install's dong.wav, ka.wav,
balloon.wav and potato.wav.

Not part of Taiko Pocket and not covered by its licence. Taikojiro's readme says its
images and drum sounds are taken from another simulator. For personal use; do not
redistribute. It is in this repository because the repository is private.

Use it when packing a library:

    python3 tools/packer/pack.py SONGS_DIR -o library.tpl --skin skins/taikojiro-original
